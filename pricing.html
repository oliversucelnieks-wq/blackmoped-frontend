Pricing questions

Is the free trial really free?+

Yes. No card needed. You get 3 free quotes per day forever to try the tool. When you're ready, pick a paid plan and your card only gets charged after a 14-day trial of the paid features.

Can I cancel?+

Any time, two taps inside the app. No retention calls, no annual contracts. If it isn't paying for itself in time saved, don't pay for it.

What if I need more than 60 quotes a month on Solo?+

Upgrade to Pro for unlimited. You can switch up or down month-to-month. We don't charge per extra quote — if you're regularly hitting the cap, Pro is better value anyway.

Do you offer annual discounts?+

Pay for 12 months upfront and get 2 months free (effectively 17% off). That makes Pro work out to £24.17/mo and Solo £11.67/mo annualised.

VAT?+

Prices shown are excluding VAT. UK customers will see 20% added at checkout. Outside the UK is reverse-charged where applicable.

Can I get an invoice for my accountant?+

Yes. Every payment generates a proper VAT invoice, downloadable from your account at any time. Tradesperson-friendly, accountant-friendly.
