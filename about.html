About

Blackmoped is a small studio building AI-powered software for UK tradespeople. We're not a venture-funded startup. We're not trying to be the next ServiceTitan. We just think the tools sparks have to use right now are mostly bloated, overpriced, and built for office staff who don't actually swing a screwdriver.

So we built one for the spark in the van.

What we believe

Software should pay for itself in time saved. If our tool doesn't save you more than £14/month worth of evening admin time, you shouldn't use it.

Sparks aren't the audience for "enterprise SaaS." No onboarding calls. No "speak to sales." No 14-tab settings panels. You sign up, set your day rate, and start quoting. Three minutes from "I just heard about this" to "I just sent a quote."

AI should do the boring bits, not the skilled bits. The AI writes line items. You set the prices, judge the job, sign the certificates. We're not trying to replace what you know - we're trying to replace the laptop time.

Where we are

Based in the UK, building from a small remote team. Servers in Frankfurt. AI provided by Anthropic via OpenRouter. Customer support handled by us, not a chatbot.

What's next

Right now: the web tool. Soon: native iOS & Android apps. After that: branded PDF generation, customer e-sign, deposit collection via Stripe, and a proper EICR template flow. If there's something you wish existed, tell us. We read every email.
