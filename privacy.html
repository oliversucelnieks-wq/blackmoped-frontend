Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 May 2026

Blackmoped ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Specifically:

  • Job descriptions you submit — used to generate quotes. Sent to our AI provider (OpenRouter / Anthropic) for processing. Not stored long-term unless you have an account.
  • Account info (paid plans only) — email address, plan tier, billing details. Card details are processed by Stripe; we never see them.
  • One essential cookie stored locally in your browser to remember your free quote count. No tracking cookies, no third-party advertising cookies.
  • Server logs — IP address, request time, and HTTP status. Kept for 30 days for security and debugging, then deleted.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We don't use your job descriptions or quotes to train AI models.
  • We don't show you ads.
  • We don't share data with third parties except as listed below.

Third parties we use

  • OpenRouter / Anthropic — to generate quotes from your job descriptions. Job text is sent over HTTPS, processed, and discarded by them per their data policies.
  • Render — hosts our backend in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.
  • Stripe (paid plans only) — processes payments. Stripe is its own data controller for payment data.

Cookies

We use exactly one piece of browser storage: a small entry in localStorage recording how many free quotes you've used today. This is essential to the service and doesn't require consent under PECR. The cookie banner exists for transparency.

If you accept, we may also store your cookie preference. If you decline, we don't store the preference (so you'll see the banner again on next visit).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any data we hold about you
  • Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these, email privacy@blackmoped.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data retention

  • Free users: nothing stored on our servers. Quotes generated in your browser only.
  • Paid users: account data and quote history kept while subscription is active. Deleted within 30 days of cancellation unless you ask sooner.
  • Server logs: 30 days, then automatically purged.

Where we store data

Servers hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). All data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS. Backups encrypted at rest.

Children

Blackmoped is for tradespeople running businesses. Not intended for under-18s. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

If we update this policy meaningfully, we'll email account holders and post a notice on the homepage for 30 days.
